Privacy Policy

Last updated August 13, 2026

Who we are

Breezy Welding is a custom welding and metal fabrication shop based in Gilbert, Arizona, serving the Phoenix metro. You can reach us any time at (480) 359-5197 or breezywelding@gmail.com.

What we collect

We only collect what you hand us. That happens in one of two places:

  • The quote form on this site. Your name, phone number, email address, and whatever you tell us about the project.
  • A form attached to one of our Facebook or Instagram ads. If you tap one of our ads and submit the form there, Facebook passes us your name, phone number, and email address.

We do not ask for payment details, and we never ask for a Social Security number, a bank account, or anything else of that sort. If someone claiming to be us asks you for that, it is not us.

How we use it

To get back to you about your project. That means calling or texting you, emailing a quote, scheduling an on-site estimate, and following up about the work. That is the whole list.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your information. Not to anyone, for any price.
  • We do not rent, trade, or hand your list to other businesses.
  • We do not use it for marketing that has nothing to do with the quote you asked for.

Who else touches it

A few services help us run the site and receive your message. They handle your details only so we can get them:

  • FormSubmit delivers the quote form to our email inbox.
  • Meta (Facebook and Instagram) runs our ads and their lead forms.
  • Vercel hosts this website.
  • Google Fonts serves the typefaces this page uses.

Each of those companies has its own privacy policy covering what it does with data on its end.

Text messages and calls

When you send us your number, you are asking us to get back to you, so we may call or text you about your project. If you would rather we stop, just say so on the call, reply STOP to a text, or email us. We will stop.

How long we keep it

Your quote request reaches us as an email and stays in our inbox. We keep it while we are working with you, and after the job so we can answer warranty questions and pick up where we left off if you come back for more work.

We do not keep it on a fixed schedule, and we will not pretend otherwise. What we will do is delete it whenever you ask.

Getting your information removed

Email breezywelding@gmail.com or call or text (480) 359-5197 and tell us you want your information deleted. You do not need to give a reason. We will take care of it and confirm when it is done.

You can use the same contact to ask what we have on file for you, or to correct something that is wrong.

Cookies

This site does not set advertising or tracking cookies of its own. If you arrived from one of our Facebook or Instagram ads, Meta may have its own tracking in play, which is governed by Meta's privacy policy rather than ours.

Children

This site and our ads are meant for adults who own or manage property. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If we change how any of this works, we will update this page and change the date at the top.

Questions

Call or text (480) 359-5197, or email breezywelding@gmail.com. We are around Monday through Saturday, 7am to 6pm.
